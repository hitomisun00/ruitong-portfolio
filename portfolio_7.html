About me

I ask "why" before I ask "how" — and that's what makes the difference.

I'm Sun Rui Tong, a UI/UX and Experience Designer from Singapore with a background in Product Design and Visual Communication from Nanyang Technological University.

My work spans healthcare, fintech, blockchain, travel, and e-commerce — across companies like Mercedes-Benz, Changi Airport Group, Shopee, and now Returning.AI, where I own not just the UI but the entire feature logic design process.

How I work

My design process

01
Ask & Discover

I start with questions — lots of them. Stakeholder interviews, user research, competitive audits. I don't open Figma until I understand the real problem, not just the stated one.

02
Map the Logic

Before designing screens, I design the system. I map feature flows, edge cases, and admin configurations — the thinking layer most designers skip over.

03
Design & Iterate

Lo-fi to hi-fi, fast. I test assumptions early, loop in developers and POs throughout, and iterate on real feedback — not hypotheticals.

04
Ship & Learn

Pixel-perfect specs with documented logic. Post-launch, I track how the design performs and feed learnings back into the next cycle — the loop never ends.
